Lion Entertainment's Macintosh DOOM (Ultimate DOOM 1.0.2), rebuilt with Retro68
for 68040 and 68LC040 Macs (Quadra 800 class; System 7.1 or later, 32 MB RAM
recommended, 256 colours).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `UltimateDOOM-<version>-68040.sit.hqx` | The application. Unstuff it next to your `DOOM.WAD`, `DOOM2.WAD`, `TNT.WAD` or `PLUTONIA.WAD`. |
| `DOOM-<version>-68040-shareware.hda.zip` | Ready-to-play SCSI disk image: the application, the shareware `DOOM1.WAD` and music. Works on emulators (QEMU `q800`, Basilisk II, MAME), SCSI emulators (BlueSCSI, ZuluSCSI) and the MiSTer Quadra 800 core. |
| `DOOM-<version>-68040-noWAD.hda.zip` | The same without a WAD, for your own IWADs and add-ons. |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums. |

Highlights: TCP/IP, serial and AppleTalk multiplayer; add-on WADs (WADs menu,
up to 32 files) with vanilla limits removed; DeHackEd and BEX patches (.deh /
.bex files and DEHACKED lumps); music played from WAD lumps; frame-rate
counter and built-in benchmark.  See the README for details.

Only the shareware WAD is freely redistributable; bring your own copies of
the commercial games.

Lion Entertainment's Macintosh DOOM (Ultimate DOOM 1.0.2), rebuilt with Retro68
for **68040 / 68LC040 Macs** (Quadra 800 class; System 7.1 or later) and, natively,
for **PowerPC G3 / G4 Macs** (Mac OS 8 or later; tested on Mac OS 9.2.2).
32 MB RAM recommended; 256 colours (the game offers to switch).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `UltimateDOOM-<version>-68040.sit.hqx` | The 68K application. Unstuff it next to your `DOOM.WAD`, `DOOM2.WAD`, `TNT.WAD` or `PLUTONIA.WAD`. |
| `UltimateDOOM-<version>-PPC.sit.hqx` | The native PowerPC application (G3/G4) — much faster there than the 68K one under the emulator. Same use. |
| `DOOM-<version>-68040-shareware.hda.zip` | Ready-to-play SCSI disk image (68K): the application, the shareware `DOOM1.WAD` and music. Works on emulators (QEMU `q800`, Basilisk II, MAME), SCSI emulators (BlueSCSI, ZuluSCSI) and the MiSTer Quadra 800 core. |
| `DOOM-<version>-68040-noWAD.hda.zip` | The same without a WAD, for your own IWADs and add-ons. |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums. |

Highlights: native PowerPC build; TCP/IP, serial and AppleTalk multiplayer;
add-on WADs (WADs menu, up to 32 files) with vanilla limits removed;
UMAPINFO (SIGIL, SIGIL II, No Rest for the Living, Master Levels); DeHackEd
and BEX patches; music converted from WAD lumps and played by QuickTime;
Options > Mouse Look (mouse turns and fires, WASD / arrows move); on large
monitors an offer to switch to 640x480 while playing; frame-rate counter
and built-in benchmark (Control menu).  See the README for details.

Music needs QuickTime 2.x or later with QuickTime Musical Instruments.

Only the shareware WAD is freely redistributable; bring your own copies of
the commercial games.
